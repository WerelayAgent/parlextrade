What a slip is

A slip is one to six price calls on one ticket. Each call says where an asset will be after a time you choose, at a price you choose. Each leg has its own odds, the slip multiplies them together, and the payout is your stake times that number. Every leg has to win.

Why it matters

Fifteen minutes or seven days. A tiny move or a moonshot. BTC, Tesla, gold and a meme coin on the same ticket, settled from real market prices and paid in USDG on Robinhood Chain. No bookmaker account, no fiat, no waiting.

Three steps to a placed slip

01
Deposit
Connect a Robinhood Chain wallet and send USDG to the platform. It shows up as your balance a few seconds after the transaction confirms.
02
Call it
Tap an asset, say Up or Down, type the price you think it will be past, and pick a horizon from 15 minutes to 7 days. The odds update as you type. Stack 1 to 6 calls, one per category.
03
Settle
Type a stake, see the payout, submit. When each leg's time is up we read the market price. If every leg is on the right side of its target, the payout lands in your balance in USDG. If one misses, the slip loses.

Six markets, one ticket

What you can pick from
  • Crypto majors like BTC, ETH and SOL
  • Famous meme coins like DOGE, SHIB, PEPE and WIF
  • Public stocks like TSLA, NVDA and AAPL
  • Penny stocks like SNDL, LCID and PLUG
  • Real-world assets: gold, silver, oil, real estate, treasuries
  • Custom pairs: any Robinhood Chain token its community has listed
  • Any target price, 15 minutes to 7 days out
What happens to your slip
  • Every leg wins: payout in USDG, credited to your balance
  • Any leg loses: the slip loses the stake
  • A leg that cannot be priced at expiry is voided and drops out of the slip
  • Withdraw USDG to your own wallet any time
